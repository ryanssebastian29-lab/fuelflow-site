Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of your data. If you can't access the app, email us at the address above with the subject line "Delete my account" and we'll do it for you.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel your FuelFlow Premium subscription at any time.

Is my dietary or health information kept private?

Yes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and how it's used.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear about it — email us with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what happened, and your device model if relevant).
