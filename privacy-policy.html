FuelFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

FuelFlow ("we," "our," or "the app") is a sports nutrition tracking app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We've tried to write this in plain language rather than dense legal text.

Who can use FuelFlow

FuelFlow is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete that account and its data.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address, used only to create your account and let you log in.

Profile information

  • Age, sex, height, weight, sport or lifestyle focus, training frequency, goals, food likes/dislikes, and dietary restrictions or allergies you choose to share. You provide this voluntarily during onboarding to personalize your experience.

Activity you log

  • Food entries (including photos you choose to upload for food recognition), sleep logs, and weight logs you enter yourself.

AI chat messages

  • Messages you send to the in-app nutrition assistant, and the assistant's replies, so your conversation history can be shown to you.

Subscription and purchase information

  • If you subscribe to FuelFlow Premium, we receive information about your subscription, such as which plan you chose, when it started, whether it is active, and when it renews or expires. We use this only to give you access to Premium features. We never receive or store your payment card details; all payments are handled by Apple.

Automatically collected information

  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app securely (such as login session data). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide core features: calorie and macro targets, personalized meal plans, food and sleep tracking, and the AI nutrition assistant.
  • Personalize suggestions based on the profile details, preferences, and activity you provide.
  • Check whether you have an active Premium subscription and unlock Premium features.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of your account.
  • Send optional push notifications (see below), only if you allow them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

Sharing your information

We share information only as needed to operate the app:

  • Supabase, our database and authentication provider, stores your account and activity data securely.
  • Groq, an AI processing provider, receives the content needed to generate AI responses (such as your chat messages, food descriptions, or photos you submit for recognition, along with relevant profile details like dietary restrictions) in order to return a result to you. This content is sent for the purpose of generating your response and is not used by us to build advertising profiles.
  • Apple processes all in-app subscription payments through the App Store. Apple's handling of your payment information is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat, our subscription management provider, receives your app user ID and your App Store purchase details (such as plan, purchase date, and renewal status) so we can confirm your subscription and unlock Premium features. RevenueCat does not receive your payment card details.

We do not share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

Push notifications

FuelFlow may offer optional push notifications (for example, reminders related to your training schedule or logging your meals). Notifications are only sent if you grant permission on your device, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct your data: You can view and edit your profile information at any time in the app.
  • Delete your data: You can delete individual food, sleep, and weight entries in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, open the app and go to Profile → Delete Account. You can also contact us at ryanssebastian29@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription: You can manage or cancel FuelFlow Premium at any time in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your FuelFlow account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription, so please cancel it there as well.
  • Data portability: You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at the email above.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure authentication and access controls that limit your data to your own account. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

AI-generated content disclaimer

FuelFlow's meal plans, food recognition, and chat assistant are powered by artificial intelligence and are intended for general educational and informational purposes only. They are not a substitute for advice from a doctor, registered dietitian, or other qualified healthcare professional. Always use your own judgment, especially regarding allergies, medical conditions, and dietary restrictions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email.
